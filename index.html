Privacy Policy / 개인정보 처리방침

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

OVERRUNE× ("the App") respects your privacy. We do not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number) on our own servers. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

The App uses trusted third-party services to provide optional rewarded/interstitial advertisements and process in-app purchases ("Remove Ads"). These services may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA/AAID), coarse location, and diagnostic/usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy & Terms
  • RevenueCat (In-App Purchases): RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Apple Privacy Policy

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, you will be asked for permission before any tracking occurs for personalized advertising. You can change your preference at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

OVERRUNE×는 별도의 회원가입 없이 이용 가능하며, 개발자 서버에 사용자의 개인식별정보를 직접 수집하거나 저장하지 않습니다. 단, 광고 제공(Google AdMob) 및 인앱 결제 처리(Apple/RevenueCat)를 위해 기기 식별자(광고 ID) 및 결제 영수증 검증 데이터가 해당 제3자 서비스의 정책에 따라 안전하게 처리될 수 있습니다.
